I'm an Identity and Access Management (IAM) professional specializing in designing, developing, and implementing end-to-end identity governance solutions using **SailPoint IdentityIQ** and **Saviynt IGA**.

My core IAM expertise includes user and role management, access reviews, RBAC, policy enforcement, provisioning/deprovisioning, and full identity lifecycle management across Joiner, Mover, and Leaver (JML) processes. I have extensive experience in application onboarding across Active Directory, JDBC, LDAP, web services, Linux Direct, Cloud Gateway, MSSQL, and SCIM 2.0 — along with deep knowledge of SailPoint modules including Compliance Manager, Lifecycle Manager, and Password Management.

I have good knowledge of **CyberArk PAS**, spanning Enterprise Vault, Privileged Session Manager, and Central Policy Manager. Beyond IAM, I have a keen interest in **Arduino and IoT**, with practical experience in embedded systems development.
